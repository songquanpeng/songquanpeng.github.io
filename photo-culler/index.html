NATIVE MAC PHOTO WORKFLOW

Keep the photos
that matter.

Photo Culler is a native macOS app for reviewing JPG + RAW shoots, rating your favorites and organizing originals into a library. Requires macOS 14 or later.

From card to library

  1. Choose Add Source and select your SD card or a photo folder. Build local previews for faster browsing.
  2. Use arrow keys to browse, 0–5 to rate, G to switch grid / preview, and Z to inspect the original JPG at 100%.
  3. Filter by rating, select your photos, then Import, Share or Delete. Confirm whether to include JPG, RAW or both.
  4. Imports copy originals to date folders in your chosen library and preserve ratings. RAW previews depend on a readable embedded preview; full-resolution RAW editing is not included.

Need help?

Report a problem or request a feature through the developer's support tracker. Include the app version, macOS version, camera model and steps to reproduce. Reports are public: do not attach private photographs, catalog backups or personal information.

Common questions

Where are my ratings? Ratings are stored locally and survive disconnecting the card, hiding a source, and clearing previews. Back them up using File → Back Up Catalog.

How do I clear previews? Open Cache & Settings with ⌘, and choose the cache to clear. Ratings and originals remain intact.

How do I reconnect a moved folder? Right-click its sidebar entry and choose Relocate Folder. Select the folder containing the same relative photo paths.

How do I change language? Choose Follow System, English or 简体中文 in Cache & Settings, then reopen the app.

原生 macOS 摄影工作流

把值得留下的照片
挑出来。

挑图为 JPG + RAW 拍摄习惯设计，支持快速预览、打星筛选和按拍摄日期整理图库。需要 macOS 14 或更新版本。

从 SD 卡到图库

  1. 添加 SD 卡或照片目录，生成本地预览，加快日常浏览。
  2. 方向键翻图，0–5 打星，G 切换网格与预览，Z 查看 JPG 原图 100%。
  3. 按星级筛选并选择照片，再导入、分享或删除，操作前确认 JPG、RAW 或两者。
  4. 导入会将原件复制到目标图库的日期文件夹，保留评分。RAW 预览依赖可读取的内嵌预览，不包含 RAW 原图解码与编辑。

常见问题

拔卡后评分还在吗？评分保存在本机，拔卡、隐藏来源或清理缓存都不会清除。可通过“文件 → 备份图库记录”另存备份。

怎样清理预览？按 ⌘, 打开“缓存与设置”，选择需要清理的缓存。星级与原件独立保留。

目录移动后如何恢复？右键侧栏目录，选择“重新定位目录”，选择内部相对路径不变的照片目录。

怎样切换语言？在“缓存与设置”选择跟随系统、English 或简体中文，重新打开应用后生效。
